Medical Image Segmentation & Detection

Two 3D medical-imaging pipelines for cardiac CT, built for the AI CUP 2025 competition: UNet3D whole-heart segmentation and YOLOv12 aortic-valve detection.

Overview

Coming from a vehicle-engineering background, AI CUP 2025 was a chance to apply deep learning to a domain outside vehicles — one that turned out to share real structure with autonomous-vehicle perception. Both segmentation and detection here work on volumetric (3D) data, and both need to find small, low-contrast targets reliably: the same underlying problem as lidar/voxel processing and small-object detection in ADAS.

The pipeline splits into two models: a UNet3D network that segments the whole heart from cardiac CT volumes, and a YOLOv12 detector tuned to localize the aortic valve — a small, low-contrast target that's easy to miss.
